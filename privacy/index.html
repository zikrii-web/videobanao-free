Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Your media never leaves your device

VideoBanao Free runs entirely in your web browser. When you upload a photo or a music file into the editor, it is processed locally on your phone or computer. It is never uploaded to any server, never stored by us, and never shared with anyone. The videos you export are created on your device and downloaded directly to it.

What we collect

  • No accounts — there is no sign-up, so we collect no names, emails or passwords.
  • No media data — your photos, audio and videos stay on your device.
  • Local preferences only — your language and theme choices are saved in your own browser's local storage, on your device only.
  • Basic hosting logs — like every website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (page requested, browser type) for security and uptime.

Cookies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The only stored values are your own display preferences (language, theme) in local storage.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving the font files, as described in Google's privacy policy. We embed no trackers, ads or analytics scripts.

Contact

For any privacy question, use our contact page.
