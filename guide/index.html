How to use VideoBanao Free

Photo se video kaise banaye — ye sawal har Shorts/Reels creator poochta hai. Is guide me hum step by step seekhenge ki kaise is free video maker se photos, text, animation aur music ke saath professional video banaya jata hai — bina koi app install kiye, bina watermark.

Step 1 — Template chuno ya blank shuru karo

Home page par 6 ready templates hain — Motivation Quote, Product Promo, Diwali/Festival Greeting, Birthday Wish, Photo Slideshow aur Business Ad. Ek tap me scenes, text aur colours bhar jate hain. Chaaho to khaali scene se bhi shuru kar sakte ho.

Step 2 — Scenes aur photos add karo

Har scene ek photo ya ek message hota hai. Scene ke Background me "My photo" chuno aur apni photo upload karo — ya bina photo ke animated gradient / solid colour background use karo. Photo par Ken Burns motion (zoom in, zoom out, pan left/right) lagta hai, jisse still photo bhi film jaisi lagti hai. Scene ka samay 2–8 second ke beech set karo.

Step 3 — Text, animation aur style

Har scene me ek bada headline aur ek chhota subtext likho. 6 fonts (Poppins, Noto Sans Hindi, Serif, Sans, Impact, Mono), koi bhi text colour, bold option aur text size control milta hai. Text animation chuno — Fade (dheere dikhe), Slide up (neeche se aaye), Zoom (bada hokar aaye) ya Typewriter (type hota hua). Scenes ke beech Fade ya Slide transition lagao.

Step 4 — Apna music lagao

Music section me apne phone se koi bhi audio file upload karo — wo exported video me apne-aap mix ho jayegi. Music nahi chahiye to "No music" hi rehne do; video silent banega.

Step 5 — Size chuno, preview dekho, export karo

  • 9:16 — YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp Status ke liye
  • 16:9 — normal YouTube videos ke liye
  • 1:1 — Instagram/Facebook posts ke liye

Preview dabakar poora video dekho. Sab sahi lage to Export Video dabao — video tumhare browser me hi render hoga (tab khula rakho) aur WebM file download ho jayegi. Exported video par koi watermark ya logo nahi hota.

Pro tips — zyada views ke liye

  • Pehle scene ke 2 second sabse important hain — sabse strong line wahi rakho.
  • Shorts ke liye total video 15–30 second rakho; har scene 3–4 second.
  • Text bada aur bold rakho — phone par chhota text nahi padha jata.
  • Apni photos hamesha original use karo; kisi aur ke video se audio mat uthao (copyright strike lag sakta hai).
